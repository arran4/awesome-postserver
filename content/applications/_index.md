Practical programs should be evaluated by the two-device test: two participating devices, no mandatory third server or always-on relay, and meaningful persisted content or state. See [Contributing](https://github.com/arran4/awesome-postserver/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for inclusion rules.

The entries here are proposals under review unless their individual pages document a qualifying mode.
